# BMW Global Sales Intelligence Dashboard (2010–2024)

## Executive Business Intelligence | Tableau

This project delivers a production-style **Business Intelligence dashboard** analyzing BMW’s global sales performance from 2010–2024. Built for executive stakeholders, the dashboard enables rapid insight into **regional growth trends, EV adoption, pricing power, and model-level performance**.

## 🎯 Business Objective
BMW operates in highly dynamic global markets facing shifts in electrification, pricing pressure, and regional demand volatility. Leadership required a centralized BI solution to:

- Identify high-growth regions
- Track EV and hybrid adoption trends
- Evaluate pricing elasticity across models
- Support strategic investment and product planning decisions

---

## 📈 KPIs & Metrics Tracked
- Total and Year-over-Year Sales Volume
- Regional Contribution to Global Sales
- EV / Hybrid Growth Trends
- Model-Level Sales Performance
- Average Price vs Sales Volume
- High vs Low Sales Classification

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Asia emerged as BMW’s strongest long-term growth market
- EV and hybrid demand accelerated significantly after 2020
- Luxury models showed low price sensitivity, indicating pricing power
- Automatic diesel configurations were most associated with high sales
- The BMW X6 demonstrated outsized recent growth momentum
